How it works

  1. Pick a strip style below, and type in a caption or date if you want one — both are optional.
  2. Press Start session and allow camera access when your browser asks.
  3. Strike a pose for each 3-2-1 countdown — three shots total, with a flash to confirm each one.
  4. Your strip builds itself automatically. Scroll the window to see the whole thing.
  5. Press Save strip to download it straight to your device.
