Photo Booth

Pick a strip below, step into the window, and strike three poses. We'll print them together when you're done.

How it works

  1. Pick a layout (photo strip or polaroid) and a style below, and type in a caption or date if you want one — both are optional.
  2. Press Start session and allow camera access when your browser asks. If your device has two cameras, tap the flip icon to switch.
  3. Strike a pose for each 3-2-1 countdown — three shots total, with a flash to confirm each one.
  4. Your print builds itself automatically. Scroll the window to see the whole thing — in Polaroid layout, tap a thumbnail to choose which shot to print.
  5. Press Save strip to download it straight to your device.
